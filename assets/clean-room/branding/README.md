# Branding

Bitmaps the title screen draws, made for the rebuild and owing nothing to the original's files.

- `dino-logo.png`: the Refurbished Dinosaurs logo, scaled from the project's white logo to 26
  pixels high so it is drawn pixel for pixel in the 640x460 drawing area.
- `restored-by.png`: the words "restored by" as wide small caps, drawn as lines 1.5 pixels
  wide at eight times the size and averaged down to 80 percent of the copyright line's letters,
  so they share its weight and grey edges.

Both are white on transparent and are tinted when drawn.
