# Branding

Bitmaps the title screen draws, made for the rebuild and owing nothing to the original's files.

- `dino-logo.png`: the Refurbished Dinosaurs logo, scaled from the project's white logo to 32
  pixels high so it is drawn pixel for pixel in the 640x460 drawing area.
- `restored-by.png`: the words "restored by" as wide small caps with strokes about 1.5 pixels
  wide, drawn as lines at eight times the size and averaged down, so they sit with the
  copyright line of the title art.

Both are white on transparent and are tinted when drawn.
