## Question

Does a report generated by the final resolution remain available and open
automatically during the surviving human's final city visit?

## Setup

As EXP-TURN-041: scenario 0, 26 turns, seed 52421, hash-verified
BLD-GOG-EN-1.1. Give roster slot 0 a separate nonrecurring Bribe in each turn from 1 to 25.
At turn 26 replace it with a nonrecurring Equip of item 40, whose cost 4
is recorded in EXP-TURN-031. The human then holds 3 cash.

## Procedure

Run with a `turn:0:2:0:0:0` order for each turn 1 through 25, then
`26:0:5:40:0:0`, and `--end-turns 26`.
Dismiss ordinary entry reports before each Done. Track Combat Results and
Last Turn Events entry and return. At match completion capture before
dismissing any final-entry report or pressing Done again. Record random
calls and extract numeric state. Omit uninitialized player-award rows,
as in EXP-TURN-041. Settled open panels count as a planning observation;
the probe need not wait for the input loop beyond the panel to begin.

## Observations

The original makes 10647 random calls. Elapsed turns remains 25, the match
is over, and the human retains 3 cash. Combat Results is entered and returns;
Last Turn Events is then entered and remains open at capture. The panel
shows one report for the insufficient-cash Equip, consistent with
RULE-EVENT-014. Player 0 has one stored Last Turn report; players 1 to 5
have none. Awards have not been initialized.

The two non-repainting BitBlt captures agree byte for byte, with marker
frame 8. The BMP SHA-256 is
684d5f27f5736329c6f67b669be508f1be6faba0bd94c17ac6ae6b43f74ef971.
Screenshots and memory remain outside the repository. Known Windows 11
white-block artifacts do not affect the observed open panel or report count.

## Results

The replay comparator checks random calls, state, reports, stored scores,
standings and outcome turn. Handler coverage uses this endpoint to compare
automatic Events entry and its return to the same final city viewer before
Done advances to awards. These checks do not compare pixels or device input.

## Conclusion

The original presents a report-bearing final entry, unlike the direct-city
endpoint of FND-UI-042. This run supports the final visit's report branch;
multiple report pages, combat-result panels and multiple human viewers need
separate original comparisons.
