## Use

The designers' description of every command, the turn structure, the objectives
and the panels. It is the best outside source for what a mechanic is for and
what the player is told about it. The file is 6,229,841 bytes and holds 30 PDF
pages scanned from 56 numbered manual pages. Citations give the numbered
manual page.

## Known errors

- It describes the Attack roll without the attacking gang's current Force,
  which the executable adds (see the combat rules).
- The Hide entry on numbered page 31 refers to a Hiding section on page 47;
  that section is on numbered pages 51 and 52.
- The Command Sequence on numbered page 45 puts the Chaos phase after Combat
  and the Transaction phase, and Terminate together with Move. The executable
  rolls Chaos before Combat, carries out every Terminate before any Move, and
  makes gangs hide during planning (RULE-TURN-002, RULE-HIDE-001).
- The detection and Crackdown formulas are on numbered page 52, in the Math of
  the Game; the executable's formulas differ from them (RULE-DETECT-001,
  RULE-POLICE-001).
- Other disagreements with the executable are recorded in the What the sources
  say section of each rule that relies on this source.
